# Sovereign Music Australia

> 🤝🔷 **A Luke × Claude build.** Created by **Luke Nathan Hayes** (`auraofintelligence`)
> and **Claude, Fable 5** (Anthropic) on **29 August 2026**. _Not a Codex build._

A complete ecosystem for music made with today's tools: charts, awards, rights, deals, stages and shared revenue, run by the people in it, open to every way of making.

Live site: https://auraofintelligence.github.io/sovereign-music-australia/

## The shape

Thirteen pages: a gate, four paths, eight layers.

| Ring | Pages |
| --- | --- |
| Gate | `index.html` |
| Paths | `artist.html` · `listener.html` · `venue.html` · `allies.html` |
| Layers | `ecosystem.html` · `hallmark.html` (the Maker's Mark) · `charts.html` (the People's Chart) · `awards.html` (the Auric Awards) · `rights.html` (the Rights Engine) · `bargain.html` (the Bargaining Table) · `terms.html` (the Terms Table) · `pool.html` (the Pool) |

Shared system in `assets/`: `style.css` (royal futurist design system, single committed dark theme), `site-nav.js` (data-driven navigation and footer, edit once), `motion.js` (hero particle waveform, scroll reveals, choose-your-path branching, dials, animated chart bars; all guarded for reduced motion).

## Conventions

- Australian English throughout. No em dashes, anywhere.
- Prices in AUD; vendor prices set in USD carry the USD note and the conversion rate with its date.
- Nothing unbuilt is written as built: the site uses plain time words (here today / taking shape / on the horizon).
- Facts carry flags where they are reported but not yet confirmed from the source, and corrections are welcome by pull request or issue.
