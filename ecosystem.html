The connections

Each layer feeds the next.

Follow the wires. Purple wires carry information; gold wires carry money back to the maker.

  • The mark feeds the chart and the billing on the stage: one record of making, read by both.
  • The chart feeds the awards: a year of member votes becomes the shortlist.
  • The rights engine backs the bargaining table and the terms table: the same quoted rules sit under both.
  • The stage and the pool return money to makers: door takings and shared revenue, with the split rules published.

Governance

Run by its members. Here is the mechanism.

Rule 01

One member, one vote

Every member holds the same single vote: artist, listener, venue or toolmaker. No weighting by revenue, follower count or catalogue size.

Rule 02

Rules change by ballot

Any change to the rules is drafted, published for members to read, then put to a member vote. The draft always comes before the ballot, so nobody votes blind.

Rule 03

Methods versioned in public

The chart method, the mark fields and the split rules live on GitHub (a public website that keeps every past version of a document and shows exactly what changed, and when). Anyone can read the current rules and every rule that came before them.

Rule 04

Contested decisions

Where a decision is disputed, the evidence considered and the outcome reached are both published. The reasoning travels with the result.

When it incorporates, the ecosystem will run on two legal shapes, one for each side of the machine. taking shape

The standards and chart side

A company limited by guarantee: the usual legal form for national member bodies. It will carry the mark, the chart, the awards, the rights engine and the two tables.

The money-sharing side

A co-operative: a business owned by the members it pays, where the surplus flows back to them rather than to outside shareholders. It will carry the pool and the shared channels.
